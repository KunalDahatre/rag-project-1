Company Employee Policy

Annual Leave

Employees are entitled to 18 days of annual leave per year.

Employees must submit their leave applications through the HR portal.

Unused annual leave can be carried forward to the following year according to company policy.

Sick Leave

Employees can take sick leave when they are unwell. A medical certificate may be required depending on the duration of the leave.

Work From Home

Employees may work from home when approved by their manager and according to company policy.

Working Hours

The standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
